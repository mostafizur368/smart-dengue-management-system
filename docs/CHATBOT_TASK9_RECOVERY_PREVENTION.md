## Safety principles
- Most people recover in 1–2 weeks, but post-dengue fatigue may last several weeks.
- The 24–48 hours after fever settles remain important for warning signs.
- Return to strenuous activity should be gradual and individualized.
- A second infection can carry higher severe-dengue risk; first infections can also be severe.
- Vaccination recommendations vary by country, age, prior infection and local policy; the chatbot does not prescribe vaccine eligibility.
- Remove standing water and protect people with dengue from mosquito bites to reduce onward mosquito transmission.
