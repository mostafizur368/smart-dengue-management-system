Encrypt a copy of your account-linked records before saving it to your private Firestore vault.
AES-GCM 256

    
Use a unique passphrase of at least 12 characters. The passphrase is not stored. If you lose it, the encrypted archive cannot be recovered. Existing Firestore records remain governed by Firebase's server-side encryption and access rules; this feature does not retroactively encrypt every existing record.
