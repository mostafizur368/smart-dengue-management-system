About Dengue

Dengue is a viral disease spread by Aedes mosquitoes. It can cause high fever, severe headache, joint pain, and skin rash. Early diagnosis and proper treatment can reduce the risk of serious complications.

🦟 What is Dengue?

Dengue is a mosquito-borne viral infection that affects millions of people every year.

🤒 Symptoms

High fever, headache, joint pain, muscle pain, nausea and skin rash are common symptoms.

🛡️ Prevention

Keep your surroundings clean, remove stagnant water and use mosquito nets or repellents.

💊 Recovery

Drink plenty of fluids, take enough rest and follow your doctor's advice carefully.

Common Dengue Symptoms

If you experience these symptoms, consult a doctor immediately.

🤒 High Fever

Sudden high fever lasting 2–7 days.

🤕 Severe Headache

Intense headache, especially behind the eyes.

💪 Muscle Pain

Severe muscle and joint pain throughout the body.

🔴 Skin Rash

Red rashes may appear on different parts of the body.

Dengue Prevention

Follow these simple steps to reduce the risk of dengue infection.

🦟 Avoid Mosquito Bites

Wear full-sleeved clothes and use mosquito repellent to protect yourself.

🧹 Keep Surroundings Clean

Keep your home and surroundings clean to prevent mosquito breeding.

💧 Remove Standing Water

Empty water containers, flower pots, and old tires regularly.

🛡️ Use Mosquito Nets

Sleep under mosquito nets, especially during the rainy season.

Recovery Guide

Follow these guidelines to support your recovery from dengue.

💧 Drink Plenty of Fluids

Drink plenty of water, oral saline, soups, and other healthy fluids to help prevent dehydration.

🛌 Get Enough Rest

Get enough sleep and avoid unnecessary physical activities while recovering.

🥗 Eat Healthy Food

Eat nutritious and easily digestible foods to support your body's recovery.

👨‍⚕️ Follow Doctor's Advice

Take medicines only as prescribed and attend follow-up checkups.

⚠️ Monitor Warning Signs

Watch for warning signs such as severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, bleeding, or unusual weakness and seek medical care promptly.
