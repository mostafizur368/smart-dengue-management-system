## Safety principles
- Encourage adequate hydration; water is the main fluid. ORS and other appropriate fluids may be used.
- Frequent small sips and small meals can help with nausea or poor appetite.
- Fluid needs are individualized. Heart/kidney disease or prescribed fluid restriction requires clinician advice.
- No specific food is presented as a cure or reliable rapid platelet booster.
- Papaya leaf evidence is limited; it must not replace clinical care.
- Persistent vomiting, inability to retain fluids, reduced urination or dengue warning signs require prompt/urgent medical assessment.
