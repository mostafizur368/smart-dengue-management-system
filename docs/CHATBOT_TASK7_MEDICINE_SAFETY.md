7. Steroids are not routinely recommended

## Safety principles
- Do not provide a personalized dose without age, weight, product concentration and clinical context.
- Do not self-medicate with aspirin, ibuprofen, naproxen, mefenamic acid or other NSAIDs.
- Do not independently stop prescribed aspirin, anticoagulants, antiplatelets or long-term steroids; urgently consult the prescriber.
- Paracetamol can be used as advised by a clinician; avoid duplicate combination products and overdose due to liver injury risk.
- Antibiotics do not treat dengue virus and are not routine unless a clinician diagnoses/suspects a separate bacterial infection.
- Children require weight- and formulation-specific professional dose confirmation.
- Emergency bleeding or dengue warning signs require urgent medical care.
